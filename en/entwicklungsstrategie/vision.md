# Vision 2030

By 2030, p2d2 should be the **leading platform for bidirectional spatial data exchange** between administration and the public in Europe.

## Long-term Goals

### Geographic Coverage

- **50+ municipalities** in Germany
- **All 16 federal states** with at least one pilot municipality
- **10+ European countries** with their own instances

### Data Categories

- **Municipal Infrastructure**: Cemeteries, parks, playgrounds
- **Transportation**: Cycle paths, parking lots, public transport stops
- **Culture**: Monuments, cultural sites, landmarks
- **Environment**: Biotopes, nature reserves, trees

### Community

- **10,000+ active users**
- **1,000+ QC reviewers**
- **100+ administrative employees** as direct users
- **Developer community** for plugins and extensions

### Technical Maturity

- **INSPIRE-compliant**: Full EU compliance
- **Federated**: Instances can communicate with each other
- **AI-assisted**: Automatic quality control and duplicate detection
- **Real-time**: Live synchronization with OSM/WikiData

## Societal Impact

### Transparency

Administrative data is accessible to all citizens and presented in an understandable format.

### Participation

Citizens actively contribute to improving public data and experience direct effectiveness.

### Efficiency

Administrations save resources by avoiding duplicate work and benefit from community corrections.

### Democracy

Open data and transparent processes strengthen trust in administration and politics.

### AI Integration

- **Automatic attribute completion**
- **Image recognition-based geometry correction**
- **Intelligent duplicate detection**
- **Predictive maintenance**: Notifications about outdated data

### Mobile-First

- **Progressive Web App**
- **Offline editing** on smartphones
- **GPS-assisted data collection**
- **Photo upload** on-site

## Sustainability

### Ecological

- **Energy-efficient infrastructure**
- **Green hosting**
- **Minimal traffic** through intelligent caching

### Economic

- **Open Source**: No license costs
- **Scalable**: Costs don't grow linearly with user numbers
- **Shared infrastructure**: Multiple municipalities share resources

### Social

- **Accessible**: WCAG 2.1 Level AA
- **Multilingual**: At least DE, EN, FR
- **Inclusive**: Low-threshold participation for all
